    How to read this, and its limits

    • Earnings: median earnings of former students (dependent students who received federal aid, working and not enrolled), 10 years after they entered, whether or not they graduated. Expected: what our model predicts from who enrolls (Pell share, age, part-time share, test scores, admission rate, type, state and similar inputs). It never sees the earnings of the college it predicts.
    • Price: average yearly net price (cost of attendance including living costs, minus grants) for aided students from families earning $0-30,000 (Affordability Gap AY2022-23). Below $0 means grants exceed the full cost on average.
    • Value-added is not proof that the college caused the difference. Some of it can come from differences between students that the data does not capture, from the mix of programs a college offers, or from noise. Use it to build a shortlist, then compare the specific programs you want.
    • Stronger option nearby ("also consider"): within 25 miles (straight line), same level (bachelor's, 2-year or under 2-year) and same kind of institution, similar expected earnings (within 10%), Pell share at most 10 points lower, no higher price for low-income families, no harder to get into (open admissions, or an admission rate at most 10 points lower), and value-added at least 10 points higher with and without race inputs. About the same: comparable colleges nearby, none stronger by that rule. Tribal, specialized, online-by-design and closed colleges are not compared, and single-gender colleges are never suggested. In a test on a different group of students (independent students, not a model input), suggested colleges also had higher earnings much more often than other comparable colleges nearby did, so the gaps aren't just noise.
    • Data describe students who entered college about 15 years ago; colleges change, close and merge. Check that a college is still open.
    • Only colleges with 500+ students, their own earnings figure, and known price and Pell share are shown (2,408 colleges). Earnings data: College Results View 2021. Prices: Affordability Gap AY2022-23.
    • Code and method: the Within Reach repository (README.md). Built for the 8th Annual Datathon for Social Good.
